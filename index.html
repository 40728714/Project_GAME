The Lost Little Boy in the Forest

Help Azai navigate the forest before it’s too late.

In "The Lost Little Boy in the Forest," you play as a young boy named Azai, who finds himself lost in a dense, mysterious forest as the sun begins to set. The clock is ticking, and Azai must navigate the forest and make the right choices to find his way home before it gets too dark. As Azai ventures through the woods, he encounters a series of decision points that will determine his fate. Each time Azai answers a question correctly, he takes a step closer to safety. However, a wrong answer leads him deeper into the forest, making it harder to find his way back home. The goal is to help Azai find his way home before the forest becomes too dark and dangerous. The game is designed with a series of branching questions where players must choose the correct path or answer the right question. Each correct answer brings Azai closer to home, while wrong answers result in him becoming more lost in the forest.
